Every template ships with the same three moves

  1. Try the language button — header chip / 🌐 icon opens the Transcript languages page inside the same side panel. Pick any track (manual or ASR) — the header updates and the lesson "re-prepares".
  2. Feel the sentence focus — no word-by-word karaoke anywhere: one full long sentence on stage, a per-sentence progress bar, loop & auto-pause around whole sentences, and an up-next queue of long sentences.
  3. Tap the ⋮ kebab — the only entrance to Settings (training unit locked to Sentence ✓, auto-pause, loops, speed, translation, mic). It is hidden until you ask for it.
